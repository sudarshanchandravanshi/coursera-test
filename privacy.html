Privacy Policy

AskSphere is a quiz-building starter site. In this initial version, quiz content is encoded into share links and quiz drafts are not sent to an AskSphere account server. Avoid entering sensitive personal information.

When advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on visits to this and other websites. Users can learn about Google's advertising practices and manage ad personalization through Google's ad settings.

Before launch, the site owner must update this policy with accurate contact details, analytics and advertising disclosures, cookie/consent practices, data retention and applicable regional privacy rights.
